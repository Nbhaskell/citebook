# Booking online

## How online booking works

You can ask for a visit in the chat on our website. The assistant checks open arrival windows, gives you a price from our price list and shows you a card with the details. Nothing is booked until you press Confirm on that card. Typing "yes" does not book anything.

## Held bookings

When you confirm, your booking is held, not confirmed. A staff member confirms it, usually within 2 business hours. A hold lasts 4 business hours. Business hours are our office hours; time when the office is closed does not count. If no one confirms the hold in time, it ends and the time is offered to other customers.

## Prices in the chat

Every price in the chat comes from our price list. A price shown in the chat is valid for 30 minutes. The call-out fee is $89 and is credited toward the work if you approve the work during the same visit. Labour is $115 per hour. In Dunmore Flats and Saltmarsh a travel fee of $40 applies.

## Jobs you cannot book online

Jobs over $300 need a written estimate before work starts, so a staff member arranges them. Jobs priced by quote are also arranged by a staff member. For sewer line repair, you can book a camera inspection online first. For slab leak or leak repair, you can book leak detection online first.

## ZIP codes we serve

Neighbourhoods we serve: Alderbrook Heights (00011), Millrace (00012), Old Quarry (00013), Tamarind Park (00014), Wren Common (00015). Outer areas, with a travel fee: Dunmore Flats (00021), Saltmarsh (00022). We do not serve other ZIP codes.

## Changing or cancelling a booking

To change or cancel a booking in the chat, give the booking reference (it starts with CB-) and the last 4 digits of the phone number on the booking. After 3 attempts that do not match, a staff member helps instead. You can change or cancel free of charge with at least 24 hours' notice. With less notice, a staff member reviews the change and the fee of $45. A booking can be moved at most 2 times in the chat. Refunds are always reviewed by a person; the chat cannot give one.

You can ask for a person at any time. For an emergency, call 555-0172.
